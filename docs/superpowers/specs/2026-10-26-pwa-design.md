# PWA: installable, full-screen, with its own icon

## Why

The owner tests every slice on their iPhone, from the preview at `https://ardoise.rznn.dev` added to the Home Screen. Three things break the "real app" feel today:

- Opening a group pops the in-app Safari sheet (URL bar, close button, Share, "Open in Safari"). There is no web app manifest, so iOS guesses the app's scope and treats `/group/…` as leaving it.
- The page does not reach the top of the screen: iOS paints its own blurred band behind the status bar.
- The Home Screen icon is a generic screenshot: there is no `apple-touch-icon` and no logo at all.

Actual Budget's PWA, installed the same way, has none of these problems. That is the bar.

Success: delete the old Home Screen entry, add Ardoise again, and every screen of every slice runs full-screen, under the status bar, with no Safari chrome, behind the chosen icon.

## Goals

- A web app manifest that makes the whole origin the app: `scope: "/"`, `start_url: "/"`, `display: "standalone"`.
- iOS standalone meta tags so content runs edge to edge under the status bar, as `docs/design.md` already intends ("Backdrops run edge to edge, under the status bar").
- An app icon, generated by the existing backdrop generator, in every size iOS and Android ask for, and the favicon replaced by the same artwork.
- Installable on Android Chrome (manifest with 192 and 512 icons is enough; Chrome no longer requires a service worker).
- The installed app picks up a new deploy by itself. iOS keeps a standalone app alive in memory for days; without this, the phone runs a stale build, and lazy-loaded routes break once the new image no longer serves the old chunks.

## Non-goals

- Offline use. No service worker, no caching. The owner ruled it out: Ardoise needs the network, and a service worker brings stale-cache problems between deploys.
- A user-selectable app icon. iOS reads the icon once, when the app is added to the Home Screen; changing it means re-adding the app. Possible later as a settings entry (all variants come out of the same generator), not now.
- iOS launch images (`apple-touch-startup-image`). One per device size, high maintenance. Revisit if the launch flash bothers the owner.
- An install prompt or "Add to Home Screen" instructions in the UI.
- Push notifications, badges, share target, shortcuts.
- An "update available" banner. The owner chose a forced reload for now; a banner (Actual Budget style) is the likely successor once friends use the app and losing a half-filled form matters.

## Anti-goals

- Any navigation inside the app opening the Safari sheet.
- Content hidden under the status bar or the home indicator. The safe-area padding already in `mobile-shell`, `group-hero` and `hero-screen` must keep working.

## Decisions

### Manifest

`apps/frontend/public/manifest.webmanifest`, linked from `index.html`:

- `name` and `short_name`: `Ardoise`.
- `id: "/"`, `start_url: "/"`, `scope: "/"`, `display: "standalone"`.
- `background_color` and `theme_color`: the light `--background` (`oklch(1 0 0)`, white) as hex. The manifest takes one value; dark mode is handled by the `theme-color` meta tags below.
- `icons`: 192 and 512 `purpose: "any"`, plus a 512 `purpose: "maskable"`.

`start_url: "/"` lets the existing routes decide: `/` redirects to `login`, and `guestGuard` sends a logged-in user on.

Caddy (`apps/frontend/Caddyfile`) already serves everything under `/srv` with `file_server`, which knows the `.webmanifest` MIME type. Check it on the preview with `curl -I`; add a header only if it comes back wrong.

### iOS meta tags in `index.html`

- `<meta name="apple-mobile-web-app-capable" content="yes">` and `<meta name="mobile-web-app-capable" content="yes">`.
- `<meta name="apple-mobile-web-app-status-bar-style" content="black-translucent">`: the page runs under the status bar, which is transparent with white text.
- `<meta name="apple-mobile-web-app-title" content="Ardoise">`.
- `<link rel="apple-touch-icon" href="…180px…">`.
- Two `theme-color` meta tags with `media="(prefers-color-scheme: light|dark)"`, set to the light and dark `--background`.

`viewport-fit=cover` is already in the viewport meta.

### Icon

The chosen artwork, picked in the brainstorm from mockups:

- Background: backdrop **violet, sunrise, seed 1**, exactly as the app renders it.
- Text: the word **`ardoise`** in lowercase, white, Figtree Variable at weight 500 (the `appTitle` weight), centred on the glyphs' actual ink bounds (not the text box), scaled so the wider of its two dimensions spans 80 % of the icon (10 % margin on each side).
- Legibility: a dark radial halo behind the text, centred: `rgba(0,0,0,.38)` at the centre, `.12` at 55 % of the radius, transparent at 80 %.
- No rounded corners, no transparency: iOS and Android apply their own mask.

The mockups cropped the portrait backdrop (860×1864) to a square with CSS `background-size: cover; background-position: 50% 30%`. The generator reproduces that crop: render the backdrop at its normal size with its normal seed, then take the square at 30 % of the vertical slack. Rendering directly on a square canvas would change the ray geometry and is not what was picked.

Outputs, committed under `apps/frontend/public/icons/` (generated, like the backdrops):

| File | Size | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `apple-touch-icon.png` | 180 | iOS Home Screen |
| `icon-192.png` | 192 | manifest, `any` |
| `icon-512.png` | 512 | manifest, `any` |
| `icon-maskable-512.png` | 512 | manifest, `maskable` |
| `favicon.ico` replaced, or `favicon-32.png` + `favicon.svg`/png link | 32/48 | browser tab |

Maskable icons may be cropped to a circle of 80 % of the side. With a 10 % margin `ardoise` would be clipped, so the maskable variant keeps the same background and halo with the text scaled to fit the 80 % safe zone (about 22 % margin). This is the builder's call within that constraint.

The generator lives with the backdrops: `pnpm --filter @ardoise/frontend backdrops` also writes the icons, or a sibling `icons` script does. It needs Figtree from `@fontsource-variable/figtree` registered with `@napi-rs/canvas` (`GlobalFonts.registerFromPath`), at weight 500.

### New deploys: forced reload

Built on the existing `/version.json` (`{"sha", "date"}`, served `no-store` by Caddy, see the preview deploy spec):

- On startup the app fetches `/version.json` and keeps its `sha`.
- Whenever the document becomes visible again (`visibilitychange` to `visible`), it fetches `/version.json` again (`cache: 'no-store'`). If the `sha` differs from the startup one, it calls `location.reload()`. No prompt, no banner: the owner's call.
- If `/version.json` is missing, unparsable, or the request fails (offline, `pnpm dev` without the file), nothing happens. `"sha": "dev"` never triggers a reload.
- Safety net: a failed lazy chunk load (Angular router navigation error caused by a dynamic import failure) triggers one `location.reload()`, guarded against reload loops (for example a `sessionStorage` flag cleared after a successful navigation).
- Caddy serves `index.html` with `Cache-Control: no-cache`, so a reload always revalidates it. Hashed bundles keep the default caching.

Where it lives: a frontend service started from the app config (`provideAppInitializer` or equivalent), next to `ThemeService` in `src/app/shared/`. It is infrastructure of the frontend, not a feature, so no screen and no building block.

### Status bar on light screens: deferred

`black-translucent` always draws the status bar text in white. Screens whose top is a backdrop (home, group page, hero screens) are fine. Plain screens in light mode (payment form, payment details) will likely get a white clock on a white background.

Out of this slice by the owner's call: the slice merges, the owner reinstalls from the preview, and a follow-up handles it only if it bothers them on the phone. iOS 26's handling of `theme-color` in standalone mode is undocumented, so the device is the only reliable source.

## Constraints

- iOS reads the manifest, the icon and the meta tags when the app is added to the Home Screen. Testing any change means deleting the Home Screen entry and adding it again.
- The preview only runs `main` (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-26-preview-deploy-design.md`), and passkeys only work on `ardoise.rznn.dev`. The phone check happens after merge; the owner accepts that, the app has no users yet.
- UI rules from `CLAUDE.md` apply to any template change: no arbitrary values, no inline styles, no component `.css` in feature templates.

## Verification

- Unit: the version check service (reload on a changed sha, nothing on same sha, `dev`, fetch failure or bad JSON; chunk-error reload fires once).
- Playwright (`apps/e2e/e2e/`): a scenario named as a use case ("app is installable") that loads `/`, follows the `<link rel="manifest">`, and asserts the manifest's `scope`, `start_url`, `display`, and that every listed icon and the `apple-touch-icon` answer 200 with an image content type.
- On the owner's iPhone, after merge, deploy and re-adding the app: log in, open a group, open a payment, add a payment, go back home. No Safari sheet at any step, nothing under the home indicator. Then deploy again and bring the app back to the foreground: it reloads onto the new sha.
- Screenshots: the icon at 180 and on a mock Home Screen in the PR, alongside the usual light and dark screenshots of any screen whose layout changes.
